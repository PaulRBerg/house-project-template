# High-performance home

Use this skill for building performance, energy, envelope, ventilation design, moisture risk, systems, operations, and related regulation. It does not invent climate inputs, a site, a specification, a compliance result, or measured performance.

## Working contract

- Identify the decision, location, jurisdiction, climate data source, project phase, available evidence, and whether the question concerns law, voluntary certification, a calculation, a product claim, or an inference.
- Separate calculated demand, equipment ratings, design targets, commissioning evidence, and measured operation. Do not promote one into another.
- For quantitative guidance, state units, boundary, method, date, source URL, and a material caveat. Recheck time-sensitive law, standards, tariffs, products, incentives, and certification requirements.
- Escalate structural, fire, hygrothermal, HVAC, electrical, geotechnical, certification, and code interpretation to responsible qualified professionals in the project jurisdiction.

## Routing

- Read [Envelope and building physics](references/envelope-and-building-physics.md) for form, insulation, bridges, airtightness, windows, shading, moisture, and condensation.
- Read [Systems and operations](references/systems-and-operations.md) for ventilation, heating, cooling, hot water, renewables, controls, commissioning, maintenance, and resilience.

Use `healthy-home-design` for health-specific indoor-air, materials, light, sleep, and acoustics questions. Return relevant options, evidence boundaries, uncertainties, and the next professional or project verification step.
