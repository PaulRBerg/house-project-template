# Envelope and building physics

Use location-appropriate climate data, applicable codes, and project-specific calculations. International reference points include the [Passive House Institute](https://passivehouse.com/) and [ASHRAE](https://www.ashrae.org/technical-resources/standards-and-guidelines), but neither replaces the project's jurisdictional requirements.

Confirm geometry, assemblies, thermal bridges, air barrier, windows, shading, moisture loads, ventilation, and construction quality before asserting performance or condensation safety.
