# Sleep, light, and acoustics

Use peer-reviewed sleep and lighting research, applicable lighting guidance, and project-specific acoustic analysis. A useful public evidence entry point is the [National Institute of General Medical Sciences sleep overview](https://www.nigms.nih.gov/education/fact-sheets/Pages/circadian-rhythms.aspx).

Do not convert laboratory findings or personal protocols directly into building requirements. State the user group, timing, exposure, measurement boundary, and uncertainty. Local noise limits and construction details require jurisdictional and professional confirmation.
