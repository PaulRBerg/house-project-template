# Materials and pollutants

Prioritize source control, verified product documentation, construction sequencing, ventilation, and flush-out planning. For broad public-health context, consult [EPA volatile organic compounds guidance](https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq/volatile-organic-compounds-impact-indoor-air-quality).

Product labels and marketing claims do not prove installed exposure or health outcomes. Confirm compatibility, emissions evidence, moisture risk, maintenance, and hazardous-material obligations for the actual project.
