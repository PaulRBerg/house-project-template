# Environmental systems

Use primary public-health, standards, and manufacturer documentation appropriate to the project jurisdiction. Useful starting points include [WHO indoor air quality](https://www.who.int/teams/environment-climate-change-and-health/air-quality-and-health/household-air-pollution), [EPA indoor air quality](https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq), and [ASHRAE standards](https://www.ashrae.org/technical-resources/standards-and-guidelines).

Treat their applicability, measurement method, occupancy, climate, source mix, and local law as project-specific. Ventilation, moisture, combustion safety, water treatment, and commissioning need coordinated design and qualified local review.
