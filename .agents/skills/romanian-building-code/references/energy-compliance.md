## nZEB as the legal floor

- **Basis: Law/regulation. Scope: General principle.** Legea 372/2005 is the statutory energy-performance framework.
  It defines nZEB as very high energy performance with near-zero or very low energy use covered by renewables under the
  statutory boundary. `verified 2026-09-21`. Source: [consolidated Legea 372/2005](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocumentAfis/285017).

  Caveat: apply the definition, exceptions and calculation method current for the permit date and building category.

- **Basis: Law/regulation. Scope: Project-specific compliance.** A grade-I building energy auditor prepares the
  design-stage raport de conformare nZEB under the Romanian methodology. The authorization documentation for an nZEB
  building must include it. `verified 2026-09-21`. Source: [Legea 372/2005, arts. 3, 10 and 17](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocumentAfis/285017).

  Caveat: the report is a design-stage assessment, not certification of the completed house.

- **Basis: Law/regulation. Scope: General principle.** Legea 372/2005 art. 17 sets renewable energy at **30% of total
  primary energy consumed**, calculated under the Romanian building-energy methodology. `verified 2026-09-21`. Source:
  [consolidated Legea 372/2005](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocumentAfis/285017).

  Caveat: it is not a share
  of electricity, installed capacity, cost or annual on-site generation.

- **Basis: Household decision. Scope: Project-specific choice.** `SITE-08` and the normative references in `brief/references.md` make Romanian nZEB the
  project's legal floor and keep compliance distinct from comfort and household preferences. `verified 2026-09-21`.
  Sources: [architecture record](../../../../brief/architecture.md) and [reference records](../../../../brief/references.md).

  Caveat: the responsible auditor and designers establish compliance.

- **Basis: Household decision. Scope: Project-specific choice.** `SITE-08` contracts no voluntary performance
  certification. `verified 2026-09-21`. Source: [architecture record](../../../../brief/architecture.md).

  Caveat: Passive House criteria in `building-science` are benchmarks only and do not replace Romanian nZEB compliance.

## Romanian calculation method

- **Basis: Law/regulation. Scope: Project-specific compliance.** Order 16/2023 approves Mc 001-2022 as the Romanian
  methodology for calculating building energy performance. Its integral annex includes the thermal envelope chapter
  and assessment of energy use by systems, with staged commencement provisions. `verified 2026-09-21`. Source:
  [Order 16/2023](https://legislatie.just.ro/public/DetaliiDocument/263984).

  Caveat: use the current annexes, inputs and
  transition rules for the actual permit and building category.

- **Basis: Law/regulation. Scope: Envelope reference values.** Mc 001-2022 §2.2.1.1, table 2.4, recommends that each
  envelope element of a new residential nZEB meet R′ ≥ R′min (U′ ≤ U′max). The reference values are: exterior walls
  **4.00 / 0.25**, windows **0.90 / 1.11**, manually operated doors **0.77 / 1.30**, roofs over the top storey (under
  terraces or attics) **6.67 / 0.15**, and ground slabs above finished grade **5.00 / 0.20**, in m²K/W / W/(m²K).
  Where the raport de conformare nZEB justifies it, Note 1 allows opaque elements to fall short. `verified 2026-09-24`.
  Sources: [Order 16/2023](https://legislatie.just.ro/public/DetaliiDocument/263984), and the Mc 001-2022 text as
  [republished by a third party](https://joriside-thermoconfort.eu/storage/media/Mc-001-2022.pdf), p. 57.

  Caveat:
  these values are corrected (thermal-bridge-inclusive) recommendations, not a compliance result. The grade-I auditor
  sets the actual layers. They supersede the older R′min 5.00 roof value of Order 2641/2017, table 1.

- **Basis: Law/regulation. Scope: Project-specific compliance.** Do not invent a single detached-house numeric threshold
  when the applicable Mc 001-2022 annex, building inputs and compliance calculation have not been retrieved. Source:
  [Order 16/2023](https://legislatie.just.ro/public/DetaliiDocument/263984), `verified 2026-09-21`.

  Caveat: function,
  climate, reference area, systems and primary-energy factors define the result. PHPP assumptions do not transfer
  without an explicit crosswalk.

## EU transition

- **Basis: Law/regulation. Scope: General principle.** Directive (EU) 2024/1275 recasts the EPBD and requires national
  transposition. `verified 2026-09-21`. Source: [official directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX%3A32024L1275).

  Caveat: the Directive alone does not identify the operative Romanian permit rule for this house.

- **Basis: Law/regulation. Scope: General principle.** Article 7 requires all new buildings to be zero-emission from
  **2030-01-01**. Until then, new buildings must be at least nZEB and meet national minimum requirements.
  `verified 2026-09-21`. Source: [Directive (EU) 2024/1275](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX%3A32024L1275).

  Caveat: check Romanian transposition, permit date and any applicable exceptions.

- **Basis: Law/regulation. Scope: General principle.** Article 10 requires suitable solar installations on all new
  residential buildings by **2029-12-31**, where technically suitable and economically and functionally feasible.
  `verified 2026-09-21`. Source: [Directive (EU) 2024/1275](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX%3A32024L1275).

  Caveat: national criteria and exemptions still control implementation. This is not evidence of a current Romanian
  detached-house PV mandate or system size.

- **Basis: Regulatory status. Scope: Transition evidence.** On **2026-07-15**, the European Commission announced formal
  notices to **all 27 EU Member States** for failure to fully transpose the recast EPBD by **2026-05-29**. Art. 17(15)
  had an earlier **2025-01-01** deadline. `verified 2026-09-21`. Source: [European Commission notice](https://energy.ec.europa.eu/news/commission-calls-eu-countries-transpose-reinforced-rules-energy-performance-buildings-2026-07-15_en).

  Caveat: this procedural status does not make Romanian building rules invalid or establish this project's compliance.

## Thermal, ventilation and fire normatives

- **Basis: Law/regulation. Scope: Thermal calculation family.** C 107-2005 is the Romanian norm family for thermotechnical
  calculation of building elements. `verified 2026-09-21`. Source: [Order 2055/2005](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocumentAfis/66784).

  Caveat: use its current amendments together with Mc 001-2022. This reference states scope, not numeric performance.

- **Basis: Law/regulation. Scope: Ventilation systems.** I5-2022 governs design, execution and operation of ventilation
  and air-conditioning installations. `verified 2026-09-21`. Source: [I5-2022](https://legislatie.just.ro/public/DetaliiDocument/264763).

  Caveat: the household's ventilation records in `brief/systems.md` may prefer category II under EN 16798-1 within this Romanian normative route. The services designer must
  calculate room flows and prove the applicable class.

- **Basis: Law/regulation. Scope: Fire safety.** P 118/1-2025 is the current general construction fire-safety norm.
  `verified 2026-09-21`. Source: [Order 267/2025](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocumentAfis/295242).

  Caveat: the fire designer identifies applicable parts, classification and project measures. No numeric limit is
  imported here.

- **Basis: Law/regulation. Scope: Building electrical installations.** I7-2011, in its consolidated form amended in
  2023, governs design, execution and operation of electrical installations associated with buildings.
  `verified 2026-09-21`. Source: [I7-2011 consolidated](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/286763).

  Caveat: the
  electrical designer determines applicable clauses and later amendments.

- **Basis: Law/regulation. Scope: Building sanitary installations.** I9-2022 governs design, execution and operation of
  sanitary installations associated with buildings, including the stated internal and site water and drainage scope.
  `verified 2026-09-21`. Source: [I9-2022](https://legislatie.just.ro/public/DetaliiDocument/262281).

  Caveat: the services
  designer must coordinate the exact public/private network boundary.

- **Basis: Law/regulation. Scope: Natural-gas supply and appliance rooms.** NTPEE-2018, the “Norme tehnice pentru
  proiectarea, executarea și exploatarea sistemelor de alimentare cu gaze naturale”, approved by ANRE Order 89/2018
  (Monitorul Oficial nr. 462/2018) and amended by ANRE Order 2/2023 (Monitorul Oficial nr. 67/2023), governs the
  distribution connection and the building's gas installation (art. 1–3). It therefore binds any gas appliance the design adds, such as a condensing boiler. The demo concept in
  `brief/systems.md` relies on a heat pump instead. `verified 2026-09-21`. Sources: [NTPEE-2018 text](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocumentAfis/201310),
  [ANRE Order 89/2018](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/201309) and
  [ANRE Order 2/2023](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocumentAfis/264378).

  Caveat: the authorised gas designer
  and the distribution operator apply the consolidated text. The earlier NTPEE-2009 (ANRE Order 5/2009) is repealed.

- **Basis: Law/regulation. Scope: Rooms with gas appliances.** NTPEE-2018 art. 128 requires, for interior gas use, a
  minimum room volume of **18 m³** (**7.5 m³** for kitchens and bathrooms), combustion air, natural or mechanical
  ventilation, complete evacuation of flue gases outdoors and glazed or pressure-relief surfaces.

  Art. 129 sizes those glazed surfaces at **0.05 m² per m³** of net room volume in masonry buildings
  (**0.03 m²/m³** in reinforced concrete). When glass is thicker than 4 mm or insulated, it requires an automatic
  gas detector acting on a solenoid valve. When detectors are fitted, it allows **0.02 m²/m³**.

  Art. 132 requires **15 m³** of room volume per m³/h of open-flame appliance flow.
  When the room volume to nominal flow ratio V_i/Q_n is at least **30**, art. 134 accepts infiltration air.
  Otherwise, it requires a direct outside-air intake. `verified 2026-09-21`. Source:
  [NTPEE-2018 art. 128–134](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocumentAfis/201310).

  Caveat: the ceramic-brick
  house (`SITE-04`) falls under the masonry glazing ratio unless detectors are used, and room-sealed condensing boilers
  have their own appliance-specific provisions. The gas designer, if gas is used at all, sizes the room and its openings on the real appliance.

- **Basis: Law/regulation. Scope: Indoor-air hygiene.** NP 008-1997, “Normativ privind igiena compoziției aerului în
  spații cu diverse destinații”, is referenced by I5-2022 and is not a gas norm. `verified 2026-09-21`. Source:
  [I5-2022 reference table](https://legislatie.just.ro/public/DetaliiDocument/264763).

  Caveat: route gas questions to
  NTPEE-2018 above.
