# Site, form, and architecture

| ID | Status | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `SITE-01` | `deferred` | Identify the site only after confirming its evidence, boundaries, constraints, access, services, hazards, and jurisdictional requirements. |
| `SITE-02` | `deferred` | Establish orientation, levels, drainage, views, privacy, shade, and surrounding context from a survey and project-specific assessment. |
| `SITE-03` | `deferred` | Select massing, storeys, roof form, materials, and architectural language through the design process. |
| `SITE-04` | `required` | Treat the 8 m × 6 m, single-storey demo shell as fictional and unaccepted; it has no plot, survey, orientation, internal plan, or compliance claim. |

`NOTE-SITE-05` A concept model cannot establish title, buildability, setbacks, utility capacity, ground conditions, approval, or construction suitability.
