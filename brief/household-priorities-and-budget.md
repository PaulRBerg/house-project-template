# Household priorities and budget

Scope: household priorities and budget policy; not render direction.

| ID | Status | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `HH-01` | `deferred` | Define household members, access needs, daily patterns, and future-change expectations before accepting a programme. |
| `HH-02` | `deferred` | Define the project location, jurisdiction, delivery route, and timeline from project evidence. |
| `HH-03` | `deferred` | Establish total project scope, exclusions, funding assumptions, and contingency policy in the budget. |
| `HH-04` | `preferred` | Compare material scope or cost trade-offs openly before changing an accepted brief requirement. |

`NOTE-HH-05` The starter budget uses fictional demo data, including an illustrative 48 m² built-area basis. It is neither a budget commitment nor evidence that this area suits a future project.
