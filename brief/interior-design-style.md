# Interior design style

| ID | Status | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `STY-01` | `deferred` | Define atmosphere, material palette, durability, repairability, maintenance, and budget priorities with samples and project evidence. |
| `STY-02` | `preferred` | Document selected finishes by performance and maintainability as well as appearance. |
| `STY-03` | `deferred` | Decide fixed joinery, loose furniture, and decorative objects only after room functions and dimensions are accepted. |

`NOTE-STY-04` Images and references can communicate intent, but do not constitute a specification or a final selection.
